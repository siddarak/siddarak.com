### Why I’m running

Heading: `Why I’m running.`

I have seen what student leadership can look like when people are heard. As General Secretary in undergrad, I listened to students, worked with administration, and helped bring events together.

That work taught me that communication is not just sending information. It is helping people find what is going on and making it easier for them to speak up when something needs attention.

There is already good work happening here. I’m running to build on it — from the student side.

### Platform

Heading: `What I’ll work on.`

**Find what’s happening**  
`On campus.`  
Make existing communications easier to find and easier to use. Students should be able to see what is coming up and share questions or changes that matter to them.

**More to show up for**  
`With each other.`  
Help create more events that students can gather around, including technical events such as hackathons. Work with clubs, CTSG, and what is already here. Add to it.

**Tell the student side**  
`Beyond campus.`  
Help more employers, neighbors, and people we meet in New York understand what Cornell Tech is through the student experience. University communications is already doing important work. Student communications can support it.

### Who I am

Heading: `About Me`

I’m a second-year MS student at Cornell Tech, studying Applied Information Science in Health Tech. I’ve been here since 2025, so I know the rhythm of campus: what students are looking for, what gets people in the room, and where communication can be clearer.

Before coming to Cornell Tech, I worked in technology consulting at ZS Associates and in presales at Montran, a payments infrastructure company. Before Cornell Tech, I studied computer engineering at NMIMS, where I was General Secretary of the student council, representing about 1,800 students. I led the university drone team and held various roles across multiple campus events.

## Debate crib

### What does the job do?

The chair handles student communications: event information, messages with Student Affairs, and planning work. I will do that job. I’m running to make those communications easier to use and more useful to students.

### Why this office?

I was General Secretary in undergrad. I know the work of listening to students, working with administration, and helping things move. Communications Chair is where I can bring that experience here.

### What would you change?

I would build on what already works. Make it easier to find what is happening, help create more things students can show up for, and help tell the student side of Cornell Tech beyond campus.

### What about the last Communications Chair?

They did good work. I’m not running against them. I want to continue that work and make it better.

### Are you promising hackathons?

No. Hackathons are one example of a technical event students may want to gather around. The work is to support events with clubs, CTSG, and the people already making things happen.

### What about employers and campus visibility?

University communications is already doing that work. Student communications can support it by sharing the student side of campus with more employers and people in New York.

### Who are you?

I’m a second-year Cornell Tech MS student. I’ve spent the last year getting to know how campus moves day to day. Before this, I studied computer engineering at NMIMS, where I was General Secretary of a student council representing about 1,800 students.
