# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone curious about Siddhesh — recruiters, potential collaborators, researchers, and people who discover him through his work. The job-to-be-done: form a clear, memorable impression of who he is and what he's building right now. The surface audience (people who land here) and the product audience (Siddhesh himself) are different; the site speaks to curiosity first, credentials second.

## Product Purpose

A personal presence site. It exists to introduce Siddhesh as a researcher who builds — not to list credentials, but to convey what he's working on and why it matters. Success means someone closes the tab remembering both the name and the work.

## Positioning

The researcher who builds: technically rigorous, actively working on autonomous drones and theorem proving, warm and human about all of it.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: get in touch (email)
- Secondary: explore projects or read his story
- The line a visitor remembers: "Building systems that see, understand, and reason."
- Belief ladder: real person → technically serious → working on something interesting right now → approachable → I should reach out
- Proof on hand: published paper at ICIVC 2023, Cornell MS (Applied Info Science + Health Tech)

## Brand Personality

Warm, curious, human. Not a developer costume — a researcher's voice. The site should feel like a note from someone genuinely excited about ideas. Anti-corporate, anti-performative.

## Anti-references

- Dark terminal / hacker aesthetic — monospace for its own sake, green-on-black, heavy CLI posturing
- Corporate LinkedIn-style resume site — timeline-as-resume, headshots in suits
- Flashy agency portfolio — cursor effects, heavy canvas, JavaScript theatrics
- Generic Next.js starter with no identity
- AI-default cream / parchment warmth — the whole near-white warm-neutral band

## Design Principles

1. **Show the person, not the resume.** Every section should feel like a human wrote it, not an LLM summarizing a CV.
2. **Currently over comprehensively.** What you're building right now matters more than the full archive. Lead with the live work.
3. **Earn depth.** Make the first fold compelling enough that visitors want to explore the rest.
4. **Restraint that has taste.** Sparse is fine; empty is not. Every blank space should be doing work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. All interactions keyboard-navigable. Every animation has a prefers-reduced-motion fallback.
